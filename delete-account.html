Delete your SafeNest account and data

SafeNest stores your backups in your own Google Drive. We keep no copy on our servers.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SafeNest.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account and data.
  3. Tap Delete everything and confirm.

This permanently deletes the SafeNest folder and every backup in it from your Google Drive, clears the backup history on your phone and removes SafeNest's access to your Google account.

Option 2: Without the app

  1. Open Google Drive and delete the folder named SafeNest, then empty it from the Drive trash.
  2. Open Google Account → Third-party connections, choose SafeNest and remove its access.
  3. If you linked OneDrive or Dropbox: delete the Apps/SafeNest folder there and remove SafeNest from Microsoft app permissions or Dropbox connected apps.

Need help?

Email tayyabniaxi@gmail.com from the Google account you used. We hold no copy of your files, so we will guide you through the steps above. We reply within 7 days.

Photos and files stored on your phone are never deleted by these steps.
