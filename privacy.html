SafeNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

SafeNest ("the app") backs up your photos, videos, files and contacts to cloud accounts you own (Google Drive, and optionally OneDrive or Dropbox). This policy explains exactly what the app touches and why. We do not run servers that store your files or personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Account basics: name, email address and profile picture of the accounts you connect, to show which account is in use.
  • Google Drive (limited): the drive.file permission only. SafeNest can see and manage only the files it created, in a folder named "SafeNest". It cannot open any other file in your Drive.
  • OneDrive / Dropbox (optional, limited): app-folder access only ("Apps/SafeNest"). SafeNest cannot see your other files there.
  • Photos and videos: read when you allow photo access, so they can be backed up. Without it you can pick items by hand.
  • Files you choose, including a WhatsApp media folder if you pick it, through the Android file and folder pickers.
  • Contacts: read only when you use Contacts backup, to save them as a vCard file in your cloud account. SafeNest never changes your contacts; restoring opens your phone's own Contacts app.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi): only while you use "Phone to phone" transfer, to connect directly to another phone. On Android 12 and older, Android requires location permission for this; SafeNest does not read or store your location.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: handled entirely by Android. SafeNest only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Where your data goes

  • Files go directly from your phone to your own cloud account over encrypted connections (HTTPS). They are not sent to us.
  • Private vault and encrypted backups are encrypted on your phone (AES-256) with a key made from your vault password before upload. We never see the password or the key and cannot recover them.
  • A list of what has been backed up, your settings and your app-lock PIN (as a one-way hash) are kept on your phone only.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your files or personal data.

Ads and purchases

  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's ads policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Premium subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Premium users see no ads.

Sharing links

If you create a share link for a file, your cloud provider makes that file viewable by anyone who has the link. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings › Delete account and data. This deletes SafeNest's folders and every backup (including the vault) from all linked cloud accounts, clears the app's records on your phone and removes the app's access to your accounts. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Children

SafeNest is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
